## Explicit acts and positions

The single source of the names used by every skill, scene list, shot list and check. Applies ONLY when `{workspace.contentPolicy.nudity}` is `allowed` and no depicted character is a minor; otherwise there is no such field.

- **Fields**: the scene list Beats columns `Sex act` and `Sex position`; the shot list lines `Sex act:` and `Sex position:`.
- **Value**: `none` (no sexual act), or `<name>[ + <name>]*[: <detail>]`. `<name>` is lower case, exactly as listed below, or `other (<plain words>)` for an act / position the list lacks. `Sex act` may combine acts with ` + ` (`blowjob + fingering`); `Sex position` names exactly ONE position (a position change is a new beat / shot). `<detail>` (optional) = who does what to whom and the contact points, in plain explicit words (`Sex position: doggy style: Lena on hands and knees on the bed, Jonas kneeling behind her`).
- **Pairing**: `Sex act` is `none` exactly when `Sex position` is `none` - except `massage` (below), which needs no `Sex position`. A one-person act pairs with a one-person position (`solo ...`), never with `other (...)`.
- **Not a sex act**: nudity alone, undressing, posing nude, kissing, hugging, caressing, touching. Nudity is the look (`<character>:nude` in Outfits), touch is `Contact` / `Blocking`; such beats and shots have `none`.
- **Sex acts**: vaginal penetration, anal penetration, double penetration, strap-on penetration, toy penetration, blowjob, deepthroat, cunnilingus, 69, rimming, handjob, fingering, titjob, footjob, masturbation, cumshot, orgasm, squirting, creampie, cum on face, cum on breasts, cum on feet, cum swallowing, spanking, tribbing, watersports, lactation, massage. Climax and finish - exactly ONE name per finish:
  - `orgasm`: a climax without visible fluid (any partner).
  - `squirting`: female ejaculation.
  - `creampie`: ejaculation inside, shown as it leaks out.
  - `cum swallowing`: ejaculation in the mouth, swallowed.
  - `cum on face` / `cum on breasts` / `cum on feet`: ejaculation landing on that part.
  - `cumshot`: visible ejaculation on any other part of the body (its `Contact:` names where it lands). There is no `facial` and no `cum on body`: those are `cum on face` and `cumshot`.
- **More acts**: `spanking` (an open hand strikes the buttocks), `tribbing` (two people grind their groins together, a thigh between the other's - pairs with `scissoring` or `lying on top`), `watersports`, `lactation` (milk leaves the breasts) - all explicit, only under nudity `allowed`, like every act here; `massage` (hands knead and stroke a back) is NOT explicit: it is read in every workspace and pairs with `prone massage` as a `Body pose:`. Not acts: `face sitting` is `cunnilingus` + the position `face sitting`; `edging` (build and stop) is a `Motion:` beat, never an act name; `hair pulling` and `choking` are `Contact:` details of the act they accompany.
- **Anatomy and gear decide who does what.** The server reads a person's genitals from the character design's `anatomy` attribute (`penis | vulva | both | none`, or free words for a non-human) and from the `gear` of the outfit the shot dresses them in (`Outfits:` pair); only when neither is set does the design's type decide (the sex of `character_type`). A strap-on named in the outfit's `gear` is the penetrating anatomy of its wearer: the shot's act is worded and judged as a strap-on, never as a penis. So:
  - A penetration by a person wearing a strap-on is `strap-on penetration` (or the plain penetration act - the server words it as the strap-on either way). Which opening follows the `Contact:`: the toucher's `groin` on the penetrated person's `buttocks` = anal, on their `groin` = vaginal.
  - `toy penetration` is a penetration with a held or worn toy (the penetrated person's or the toucher's outfit `gear` names it: `glass dildo`, `vibrator`); the toucher's hands on the penetrated person's `groin` / `buttocks`.
  - An ejaculation (`cumshot`, `creampie`, `cum on ...`, `cum swallowing`) needs a person with their own penis: a strap-on never ejaculates.
  - A character whose body differs from their type (carries both anatomies, or none) has `anatomy` set on the character design - state it only when the story depends on it; leave it out for the usual body.
  - Both acts are gated like every act: only when nudity is `allowed` and all depicted are adults.
- **Roles of a position**: the server reads who does what from the shot's FIRST intimate `Contact:` entry (one touching the groin, buttocks or breasts): its toucher is the position's first role - the carrier of `standing carry`, the one on top in `missionary`, the one behind in `doggy style`, the mouth of an oral act - and the touched person the second (a penetration written the other way round is corrected from the people's anatomy: declared `anatomy`, a worn strap-on, else the designs' sexes; an instrument act - `strap-on penetration`, `toy penetration` - from who has the gear). So write that contact toucher first (`<carrier> (hands) on <carried> (buttocks)`); without an intimate contact in frame the roles come from the people's anatomy (or sexes) only, and a person named by `Watcher:` is never one of them, and when those cannot decide them the position's limbs are not stated.

- **Modifiers**: a position takes a fixed list of modifiers in parentheses - `Sex position: <name> (<modifier>, <modifier>)[: <detail>]` - instead of a new name for each variant; at most 4, each from the entry's own vocabulary (the server refuses any other and names the accepted ones). Groups: torso (`chest down`, `hands down`, `wrists above head`), legs (`legs raised`, `legs wrapped around waist`, `legs over elbows`, `knees to chest`, `legs around hips`, `thighs around waist`, `hips raised`, `legs spread`), hold (`held by buttocks`, `held by thighs`, `held by elbows`, `wrists pinned`), actor posture (`partner lying`, `partner kneeling`, `partner seated`), `clothed` (the people stay dressed, clothes only pushed aside), and a fixture - ONE short plain noun, at most 4 words: `against <thing>`, `on <thing> edge`, `seated on <thing> edge`. Examples (any places): `standing carry (legs over elbows, against glass)`, `edge of surface (seated on counter edge, thighs around waist)`, `doggy style (chest down)`, `missionary (knees to chest, wrists above head)`. Pick the modifier that matches the staging; an arrangement no modifier covers is `other (<words>)`. The modifiers change what the server states and checks limb by limb. The scene list cell takes the same value.
- **Act / position LoRAs**: curate them like any LoRA ("Curated LoRAs": infer from name and description which ones serve the act and position, rank them high) and name the act and position EXACTLY from the lists above. If the catalog tags LoRAs with `sexActs` / `sexPositions`, the server adds a matching tagged LoRA to storyboard takes when your `LoRAs:` line has none that covers the shot.
